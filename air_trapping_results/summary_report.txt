============================================================
AIR TRAPPING ANALYSIS REPORT
============================================================

SUMMARY
----------------------------------------
This analysis quantifies air trapping in paired inspiratory/expiratory CT scans.
Air trapping is defined as lung regions with HU < -856 on expiratory CT.

KEY METRICS
----------------------------------------
1. Air Trapping Index (ATI): Percentage of lung volume with HU < -856 on expiration
2. Normal range: ATI < 10%
3. Clinical significance:
   - <10%: Normal
   - 10-20%: Mild (may be normal variant)
   - 20-30%: Moderate (consider clinical correlation)
   - >30%: Severe (likely abnormal)

OUTPUT FILES
----------------------------------------
1. air_trapping_mask.nii.gz - Binary mask of air trapping regions
2. lung_mask.nii.gz - Binary mask of lung tissue
3. air_trapping_statistics.csv - Quantitative measurements
4. slice_*_analysis.png - Representative slice visualizations
5. histogram_analysis.png - HU distribution analysis
6. 3d_visualization.png - 3D distribution of air trapping

TECHNICAL NOTES
----------------------------------------
1. Registration: Images were aligned using physical DICOM coordinates
2. Lung segmentation: Threshold-based (-1000 to -400 HU) with noise removal
3. Air trapping definition: Standard clinical threshold of -856 HU
4. Results should be interpreted in clinical context
